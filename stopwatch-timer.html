What Is a Stopwatch & Timer?

This tool combines two time-tracking utilities: a stopwatch for timing how long something takes (with lap tracking), and a countdown timer that counts down from a set time and alerts you when it finishes.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Switch between Stopwatch and Countdown using the tabs.
  2. For the stopwatch: press Start, use Lap to record split times, and Reset to clear.
  3. For the countdown: set hours, minutes and seconds, then press Start.

Benefits of Using ToolKit Pro's Stopwatch & Timer

  • Two tools in one — stopwatch and countdown timer.
  • Lap tracking — record split times during a stopwatch session.
  • Custom countdown — set any hours, minutes and seconds.
  • Free and instant — no app download needed.

Why Use ToolKit Pro?

Whether you're timing a workout, a study session, cooking, or a presentation rehearsal, ToolKit Pro's Stopwatch & Timer gives you a simple, precise, ad-free tool that works instantly in your browser.

Real-Life Examples

  • Timing laps during an interval workout.
  • Setting a 25-minute countdown for a focused study session.
  • Timing how long a cooking step takes with lap tracking.

Helpful Tips

  • Keep the browser tab open and active while the countdown runs, since background tabs can be throttled by some browsers.
