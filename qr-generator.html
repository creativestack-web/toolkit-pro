What Is a QR Code Generator?

A QR code is a scannable square barcode that stores data — like a website link, plain text, an email address, or WiFi login details — for instant access when scanned with a smartphone camera. ToolKit Pro's generator creates a QR code the moment you enter your content.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Choose a QR type: URL, Text, Email or WiFi.
  2. Fill in the relevant fields (like a website address or WiFi password).
  3. Adjust the size and error correction level if needed.
  4. Your QR code appears instantly — download it as a PNG or copy the data.

Benefits of Using ToolKit Pro's QR Code Generator

  • 4 QR types — URL, text, email, and WiFi.
  • Instant preview — see your QR code as you type.
  • Free PNG download — no watermark, no sign-up.
  • Adjustable size & error correction — customise for your use case.

Why Use ToolKit Pro?

QR codes are useful for sharing links on printed materials, business cards, restaurant menus, or event flyers, and for letting guests join your WiFi without typing a password. ToolKit Pro makes generating one instant and free, with no account required.

Real-Life Examples

  • Adding a QR code to a business card that links to your website.
  • Printing a WiFi QR code so guests can join your network by scanning instead of typing a password.
  • Sharing an email QR code on a poster so people can message you instantly.

Helpful Tips

  • A higher error correction level makes your QR code more resistant to smudges or partial damage, at the cost of a slightly denser code.
  • Test your QR code with a phone camera before printing it at scale.
