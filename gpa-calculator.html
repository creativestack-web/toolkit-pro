What Is a GPA Calculator?

GPA (Grade Point Average) is a standard measure of academic performance, calculated by weighting each course grade by its credit hours. ToolKit Pro's calculator lets you add as many subjects as you need and computes your cumulative GPA on a 4.0 scale instantly.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter a subject name (optional, for your own reference).
  2. Select the letter grade you earned.
  3. Enter the credit hours for that subject.
  4. Click '+ Add Subject' to add more courses.
  5. Your cumulative GPA updates instantly as you add subjects.

Benefits of Using ToolKit Pro's GPA Calculator

  • Unlimited subjects — add as many courses as you need.
  • Standard 4.0 scale — matches most college grading systems.
  • Instant recalculation — updates live as you add or edit subjects.
  • Free and private — no sign-up, nothing sent to a server.

Why Use ToolKit Pro?

Whether you're planning your semester, checking your standing before finals, or estimating your GPA after adding a new course, ToolKit Pro's GPA Calculator gives you an accurate, instant answer without manual weighting math.

The GPA Formula

  • GPA = Σ(Grade Points × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(Credit Hours)
  • Each letter grade corresponds to a grade point value (e.g., A = 4.0, B = 3.0), which is multiplied by that course's credit hours, summed across all courses, then divided by total credit hours.

Real-Life Examples

  • A student with an A (4.0, 3 credits) and a B+ (3.3, 4 credits) has a GPA of (4.0×3 + 3.3×4) ÷ 7 ≈ 3.61.
  • Estimating your semester GPA before grades are finalized.
  • Checking how a new course grade will affect your cumulative GPA.

Helpful Tips

  • Courses with more credit hours have a bigger impact on your overall GPA, so weight your attention accordingly when planning your semester.
